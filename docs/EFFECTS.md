# Native effects passthrough

Both clients remain required. Benilla requests bullet impacts at its solid-world raycast position and grenade effects at its simulated detonation position. IW4L resolves the equipped weapon's real impact-table cell or `frag_grenade_mp` explosion definitions and runs its existing effect simulator. Effect textures and evaluated sprite/model geometry come from the player's MW2 install at runtime; no retail assets are packaged.

Effects use Warcraft's absolute coordinates converted to IW4 inches, separately from the hidden guest map. An FX-only camera makes billboards face the Warcraft view without moving the guest player or changing gun animation. The old hidden-map frag visual and sound are suppressed; the host detonation request plays the native grenade explosion alias once, listener-local (distance attenuation is not yet bridged). Complete effect frames are published by atomic replacement at up to 60 Hz; textures are cached separately and namespaced per guest process. Sprite, mark and model-debris blend modes come from authored IW4 render states, rather than the opaque default of the lit-model wrapper.

Benilla exports nearby loaded terrain/WMO/doodad collision triangles for active impacts. IW4's particle bounce, impact and trail evaluators use continuous moving-AABB/triangle tests on that geometry, with a cell index for broad-phase filtering. Snapshots cover twelve-yard neighborhoods around up to sixteen recent impacts, capped at 32,768 triangles. Outside that snapshot there is no hidden-MW2-map collision fallback. Convex-only colliders are not yet exported, and moving collision snapshots refresh on new impacts rather than every frame.

Native immediate impact-mark textures, size, orientation and tint are projected and clipped to the actual Warcraft triangles. Marks are capped at 128 and retire after thirty seconds. This is surface decoration, not deformable or destructible Warcraft terrain. Impacts currently use IW4's dirt surface cell; full per-material surface classification is pending.

This is a first integration, not complete coverage of every IW4 shader/effect lane. The native code-mesh sprite/trail and FX-model lanes are bridged. Specialized GPU particle-cloud/fountain draws, dynamic effect lights, screen-space/camera-shake effects and marks emitted later by secondary particle collisions are not yet bridged. Gameplay damage remains on the already implemented server path and is not changed by visual-effect requests. Attended testing is required for appearance, mark projection, debris contacts and sustained-fire performance.

Frag audio resolves the weapon's projectile sound when present, otherwise the retail `grenade_explode_default` FX/notetrack alias. IW4's loaded-sound catalog preserves ordinary following-pointer slots and alias chains, not just inserted pointers; this is required to connect the grenade aliases to their already loaded retail PCM. A one-time metadata-only diagnostic is written to the private bridge when the sound bank is ready.
