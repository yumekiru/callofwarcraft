# Frag passthrough

IW4L selects `frag_grenade_mp` and owns the real pullback, cooking and throw animations. Its native launch computes velocity, remaining fuse and authored bounce coefficients; grenade inventory is not consumed in CoDCraft mode. Bullet magazines remain finite, with unlimited reserve ammo.

The guest writes a versioned launch record in the private bridge's `*.grenades` directory. Benilla freezes the guest-to-Warcraft translation and linked-camera rotation at launch, then advances the frag with swept sphere casts against loaded terrain, WMO and doodad collision. The inverse rotation is applied to position feedback. It uses the native gravity and incidence-weighted bounce formula, settles slow ground contacts and holds flight if the ground collider has not loaded. The hidden MW2 map is not a fallback collision world. The real M67 world mesh/materials are exported at runtime from the user's MW2 installation, not bundled with this repository.

Benilla returns the grenade's live position to IW4L and sends separate launch/explosion events to the local Warcraft server. The server checks sequence, fuse, elapsed time, map, travel bounds, radius, VMAP line of sight and terrain obstruction before damage. Each accepted explosion is consumed once. Blast damage is one equipped-main-hand roll scaled from eighteen times at the center to six times at the edge, with armor mitigation (twice the previous 9-to-3 balance). A nearby owner can also take damage. Kills retain normal server credit and remote auto-loot eligibility; confirmed damage uses the existing damage-number/hitmarker path without starting melee autoattack. Accepted blasts log nearby, cover-blocked and damaged target counts for troubleshooting.

These are CoDCraft-specific client/server extensions, not features of unmodified vanilla clients or servers. Requires all three rebuilt components. Crouch/prone correctness is deferred to the full player-model port. The packet layout and bounce formula have automated tests; gameplay behavior still requires attended testing, particularly slopes, building interiors, cooked frags and repeated throws. Native weapon explosion sounds remain on the MW2 side; the first native sprite/model/impact-mark rendering bridge and its remaining limits are described in [EFFECTS.md](EFFECTS.md).
